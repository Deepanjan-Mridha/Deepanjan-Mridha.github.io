I am an agricultural and environmental science researcher currently serving as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Washington State University (WSU) in the Department of Crop and Soil Sciences, based at the Pullman campus.

Throughout my academic career, I have dedicated myself to understanding and solving critical challenges in agriculture and environmental sustainability. My foundational expertise lies in plant nutrition, heavy metal toxicity, and developing innovative methods for bioremediation.

Currently, my research is deeply focused on the management of acidic soils in the Pacific Northwest. I am working to optimize soil conditions specifically for crucial regional crops like wheat and canola. By integrating traditional soil science with cutting-edge approaches like nanotechnology, I aim to mitigate greenhouse gas emissions, reduce toxicity (specifically arsenic and cadmium), and pave the way for sustainable, high-yield agriculture in changing environmental conditions.

Research Focus

Exploring the intersection of soil chemistry, plant biology, and advanced materials to ensure food security and environmental safety.

01

Acidic Soil Management

Optimizing pH and nutrient availability in the Pacific Northwest to maximize yields and resilience. My focus is particularly on creating ideal environments for wheat and canola cultivation.

By analyzing soil horizons and buffer capacities, we implement targeted liming and nutritional interventions that restore soil vitality.

02

Heavy Metal Toxicity

Investigating plant nutrition dynamics under environmental stress. A core focus is developing specific mitigation strategies for arsenic and cadmium contamination in agricultural soils.

By understanding the uptake mechanisms at the root level, we can design interventions that block toxic accumulation in the edible parts of the crop, ensuring food safety.

Bioremediation

Utilizing biological agents and green technologies to safely restore contaminated soils. We harness natural soil microbiology to break down or immobilize pollutants, maintaining vital ecosystem services for future planting seasons.

Nanotech & GHG Mitigation

Applying cutting-edge nanomaterials to achieve sustainable agriculture goals. We explore novel methods for targeted nutrient delivery and the mitigation of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions in modern, intensive farming practices.
